# VoiceShield AI Deepfake & Anti-Spoofing SOC — Marketing Email & Opt-Out Audit
**Project:** `voice-shield` | **Date:** 2026-10-11

## 1. Email Compliance & Dispatch Controls
- **Marketing Dispatch Status:** Security incident alerts and system notices only; no cold marketing.
- **Unsubscribe Protocol:** One-click RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe` headers and persistent unsubscribe link in every commercial message.
- **Suppression Synchronization:** Global suppression list checked synchronously before any email dispatch.
- **Sender Identification:** Real legal company entity and physical postal address included in all commercial footers.
